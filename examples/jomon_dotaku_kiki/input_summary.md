# Example Case: Jomon / Dotaku / Kiki Hypothesis

This is a worked example for Epistemic Gap Mapper.

## Central hypothesis

Modern Japanese paternal-lineage asymmetry, the discontinuity of the Yayoi-period dotaku ritual, and Kiki mythic structures may be examined as potentially connected traces of Late Yayoi social reorganization involving Jomon-associated paternal lineages.

## Intended status

This is a working hypothesis, not an established theory.

## Core A-level foundations

- D1a2a/D-M55 persists at high frequency in modern Japanese male lineages.
- Dotaku ritual flourished and then ended in the Late Yayoi period.
- The Kojiki and Nihon Shoki contain narratives of heavenly descent, land cession, land creation, and agricultural-origin motifs.

## Core B-level contribution

The hypothesis asks whether these otherwise separate phenomena can be connected as a cross-domain abductive model of Late Yayoi social reorganization.

## Core C-level vulnerabilities

- Whether any Jomon-associated group exercised military dominance.
- Whether dotaku discontinuity was caused by social reorganization rather than internal ritual change.
- Whether Kiki mythic structures preserve ritual-political memory.
- Whether Shandong/Haidai genetic changes contributed to Yayoi formation through specific migration processes.

## X-level risks to avoid

- Saying the dotaku sphere was definitively immigrant.
- Saying mythology records history directly.
- Saying comparative cases prove the Japanese case.
- Saying Y-chromosome frequencies alone prove conquest.
