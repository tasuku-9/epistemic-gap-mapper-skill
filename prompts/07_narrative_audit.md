# Prompt 07 — Narrative Audit

```text
For each target narrative, audit its relation to my hypothesis.

Output:

1. Narrative statement.
2. Component claims.
3. Source status.
4. Evidence trace.
5. Citation debt: low / medium / high / unknown.
6. What my paper accepts.
7. What my paper challenges.
8. What my paper reframes.
9. Challenge type:
   - data
   - interpretation
   - narrative integration
   - disciplinary habit
   - AI-frequency narrative
10. Safer wording for the manuscript.

Rules:
- Do not assume the narrative is false.
- Do not assume the narrative is true merely because it is common.
- Identify whether the conflict is with papers or with the narrative built around them.
```
