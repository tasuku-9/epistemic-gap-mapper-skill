# Prompt 01 — Extract Claims from Manuscript

```text
Below is my manuscript or hypothesis draft.

Do not evaluate it yet. Do not tell me whether it is true or false.
Extract its claim structure only.

Output:

1. Central Hypothesis
   - One sentence.

2. Main Claims
   - C1, C2, C3...

3. Evidence Claims
   - E1, E2, E3...
   - Claims that are based on existing data or sources.

4. Inference Claims
   - I1, I2, I3...
   - Claims that infer something from evidence.

5. Speculative Claims
   - S1, S2, S3...
   - Claims that are not yet assertable but may be testable.

6. Assumptions
   - A1, A2, A3...
   - Premises needed for the argument to work.

7. Dangerous Overclaims
   - Sentences or phrases that may exceed the evidence.

Rules:
- Do not reject a claim merely because it differs from consensus.
- Do not collapse my hypothesis into a standard narrative.
- If a claim is implicit, label it as implicit.
```
