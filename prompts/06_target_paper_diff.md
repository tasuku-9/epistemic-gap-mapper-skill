# Prompt 06 — Target Paper Diff

```text
For each target paper, compare it with my hypothesis.

Output for each target paper:

1. What the target paper directly claims.
2. What my paper accepts from it.
3. What my paper does not challenge.
4. What my paper extends.
5. What my paper potentially conflicts with.
6. Conflict type:
   - direct contradiction
   - scope gap
   - frame conflict
   - overextension
   - methodological mismatch
   - no conflict
7. Safer wording for the manuscript.

Rules:
- Separate direct contradiction from frame conflict.
- A paper not addressing my question is a scope gap, not a contradiction.
- Do not treat standard narrative as the paper itself.
```
